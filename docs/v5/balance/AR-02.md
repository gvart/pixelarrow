# AR-02 balance: lairs as assault targets

Story AR-02 (design/gdd/campaign-arc.md R2, F1, F3; field-works.md R4.4, R6). `npm run balance` (200 seeds) ran on `v5` (9b954e6, with AR-03) before the change and on the AR-02 branch after it. The new rows are printed under "Campaign arc" (`src/dev/arcBalance.ts` `assaultRows`). Scratch runs added 400-fight samples and knob probes.

## What ships

- **Knobs** (`src/data/arc.ts`, new): `GARRISON` (base 10, danger 6, level 2 + round(danger × 3 + days × 0.04), regrowth 24 h), `ARC_LAIRS` 6, `ASSAULT` (2 gates of 3 cells, 1 rampart row).
- **Garrisons** (`src/world/world.ts`):
  - `garrisonFull` (F1, 10–16 men) and `garrisonSize` (F3: one man back every 24 h since the last loss).
  - `garrison(id)` is the garrison as a band (for the power plate and `partyArmy`): the lair's band kind and culture, at the lair tier, so it always has a captain. Bandit camps and pirate coves swarm, Galatae camps rush (R4). It is never a beast.
  - `loseGarrison` keeps what has regrown and restarts the clock. It also re-rolls the men, so a captain who fell has been replaced on regrowth (R2.5). `weakenGarrison` (EV-01's event effect) now goes through it.
  - `razeLair` and `lairFallen`: a fallen lair sends out no new bands, and its bands already on the map stay (R2.4).
- **The assault army and outcomes** (`src/game/campaign.ts`):
  - `garrisonEnemy(lairId)` is the garrison army plus `garrisonWorks()` (2 free stakes, keyed to the lair) and `holdField`, so a garrison never retreats (R4, V5-024). `EnemyArmy.holdField` is new (`src/game/enemy.ts`).
  - `assaultSite(lairId)` is the lair's own ground with `assault: true`.
  - `afterLairAssault` settles the captain, bounty, event bonus and aftermath exactly as for a band (`afterPartyBattle`).
    - A victory razes the lair and puts its hoard (AR-03's `lairHoard`, on the pity counter) first in the loot, every piece an extra pick.
    - A defeat or retreat leaves the lair standing, with `lost += dead`.
  - `razeLair` also covers a garrison with no men left, which falls without a battle.
- **The assault field** (`src/world/battlefield.ts`): `BattleSite.assault` makes `generateBattlefield` add the defences:
  - a palisade row across side 1's deployment front (row 11 of 36), WORKS_OUT + 0.5 inside the zone edge, so the garrison's stakes go 2 paces before each gate and still inside its zone;
  - 2 seeded gates of 3 cells, one in each half of the dry land;
  - a rampart row behind the palisade;
  - open ground in front of the gates;
  - no river, and the strip outside the wall no higher than the rampart.

  A field battle is generated exactly as before.

## Findings for the designer

1. **The GDD's model of the walls doesn't hold.** It expected the walls to give the garrison +5 to +12 pts over open ground. They cost it 15 pts: on open ground, at equal power, a garrison beats its attacker 76% of the time, and behind its walls only 61%. The garrison's own doctrine fights its walls:
   - rush garrisons (Galatae) sally through two 3-cell gates, a few men at a time;
   - swarm skirmishers deploy at the zone's edge (src/sim/battle.ts `autoDeploy`), which is outside the palisade.

   The headline row still lands in band, because swarm and rush garrisons beat their power plate (24% at 1.0× on open ground). A garrison that held its walls would push the assault far below 38%. That bot behaviour lives in src/sim/ai.ts, which isn't AR-02's file. It goes to **EC-05** (bot AI): a garrison should hold behind its palisade, and swarm skirmishers should form up inside it.
2. **The stakes recheck (FW-01, decision 6).** With 2 paces between the stakes and the gate, the stakes are worth about 1–2 pts against Companions (31% with them, 33% without), which is noise. They're worth 1 pt against foot (39% against 40%). Riders still break on them (3.7 breaks a battle), but the palisade already funnels the charge, so the stakes add little. GARRISON_STAKES stays at 2, because AC2 names it. If the 1.0× row drifts below 38%, the GDD's first lever (GARRISON_STAKES 1) gives +2 pts and gates of 4 give +4.
3. **Fallen lairs stop spawning, and that moves 5 AR-03 rows.** `lateBands(…, 2 fallen)` now draws only from the 4 standing lairs, so their bands come from different places. Rare set pieces 350 → 349 bands (0.08 each, unchanged); tier-3 band power ×1.17 → ×1.18; the even army vs tier 3 28% → 18% (50 fights, bands 10.3 → 11.5 men); loot resale 479 → 493 g. That is the R2.4 rule working, not a regression. The share row is unchanged (93%).

## Ladder, online and lair parity

A scratch harness hashed 286 battles and states before and after the change, and the results were **bit-identical**:

- all 50 ladder floors × 4 seeds through `ladderSetup` (setup and battle hashes);
- hydra, cyclops, Nemean lion and minotaur × 6 seeds (`runBeast`);
- 40 generated fields;
- 10 online battles (`onlineBattleSetup`);
- 6 campaigns advanced 10 days;
- 6 day-60 worlds' spawns.

Online, ladder and duel sites never carry `assault` (a test checks all 50 floors), and only `garrisonEnemy` sets `holdField` or `works` on a campaign enemy (a test checks that field bands carry neither).

## Save safety

There is no bump. Lair state uses v6's `WorldSave.lairs` (`fallen`, `lost`, `lostAt`), which the save sanitizer already keeps. A test loads the v6 fixture (every lair standing, at full strength), razes one lair and beats back an assault on another, round-trips the save, and gets the same garrisons. The garrison is derived (men, captain and level), so nothing new is stored. Live saves have empty `lairs`, so every lair stands at full strength.

## Decisions (made while the user was asleep)

1. **`assault` is a `BattleSite` flag**, not the GDD's third argument. That way `campaignSetup`, which isn't AR-02's file, builds the assault field from `pending.site` with no change, and an abandoned battle replays it.
2. **No new LairState field.** A captain's death is not stored, because the save sanitizer would drop the field. Every loss re-rolls the garrison from the time of the loss, so a dead captain is "promoted" away (R2.5). A surviving captain is re-rolled too.
3. **The hoard is put first in the loot, with +1 pick per piece**, like the captain's weapon, so the Results screen shows it with no new UI.
4. **No river on an assault field.** The palisade is the obstacle, and a river through the gates would double it.
5. **The `host_musters` flag isn't set yet.** It would make the event card due with no host to follow it. AR-04 sets it.
6. **TIER3_RULES and LAIR_SET stay where AR-03 put them.** Moving them into arc.ts would be a refactor with no behaviour change.

## UI and wiring handoffs (not AR-02's files)

- **`src/game/campaignBattle.ts` / `src/state.ts`** (FW-03 or WLD-02):
  - `campaignSetup` must pass `works: pending.enemy.works, holdField: pending.enemy.holdField` on side 1;
  - `PendingBattle` needs `lairId?: number`;
  - `settleBattle` should call `camp.afterLairAssault(lairId, outcome, sim.result(), enemyHeroes)` when it is set.

  Until then no lair assault can start in the app, so nothing changes for live players.
- **WLD-02** (Assault sheet, arc chip, burnt lair):
  - tapping a lair: `world.garrison(id)` gives size, power, captain and doctrine; `camp.garrisonEnemy(id)` and `camp.assaultSite(id)` give the battle;
  - `world.garrisonSize(id) === 0` means `camp.razeLair(id)`, with no battle;
  - the burnt state is `world.lairFallen(id)`;
  - "Lairs n/6" is `world.lairsFallen` / `ARC_LAIRS`.
- **EC-05**: garrisons hold their walls, as in finding 1.
