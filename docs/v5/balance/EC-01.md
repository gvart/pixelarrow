# EC-01 balance: the forge reroll (the endgame gold sink)

Story EC-01 (`design/gdd/forge-reroll.md`). `npm run balance` (200 seeds) ran on
the EC-01 branch after the change (1190 s on a loaded machine). The forge
touches no battle, AI, item-table or loot code, so no battle row can move.

## Anchors (after)

| Row | Measured | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matched battle, starting army vs matched bot | **53%** | unchanged (about 53%) |
| Rarity mirror L5 / L10 (targets 58 / 66 / 76 / 85) | 61/60, 63/68, 78/75, 86/84 | inside ±5 of each target; no item code changed |
| Loot resale per tier-3 win (4 picks) | 493 g, battle pay 218 g | the faucet the sink answers (AR-03) |

## The sink row

| Row | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One reforge, tier-3 legendary | 240 g | `FORGE`, `tests/forge.test.ts` |
| One reforge, tier 1–3, uncommon to legendary | 20–240 g | GDD rule 6 table |
| Expected gold to put Keen at 2 steps on a tier-3 legendary falcata (3 other lines held) | **960 g** (4 tries) | `tests/forge.test.ts` "gold sink" |
| That, in tier-3 wins | 4.5 wins of battle pay; 1.3 wins of pay plus resale | 213–218 g pay, 493 g resale |
| One legendary kit for one man (20 lines) | about 20 000 g or more | GDD "Sink capacity" |
| Sink capacity of a 20-man army | not reachable in any campaign | 100 items, up to 4 lines each |

**Verdict: late-game gold now has an unbounded, optional use.** One reforge is
under one tier-3 win's gold, so the forge is usable from the first tier-3 win;
perfecting kit takes many wins, so gold no longer piles up without a use. The
forge sells no gold back (value is unchanged by a reforge), so it is no loop.

## Power ceiling (scratch run, not part of `npm run balance`)

The rarity mirror with side 0's kit changed, 100 seeds:

| Kit (side 0) vs side 1 | L5 | L10 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural legendary vs common | 85% | 84% |
| Every line at 2 steps vs natural legendary | 62% | 60% |
| Best stats of each pool at 2 steps vs natural legendary | 69% | 66% |
| Best stats at 2 steps vs common | 97% | 93% |

A perfected item adds about **3.5 points** over a natural one, inside the
10-point item cap (V5-071). A perfect kit beats common gear above the 85%
legendary target, which describes an average drop; a perfect natural drop
reaches the same ceiling. Campaign gear never enters duels or the war map.
