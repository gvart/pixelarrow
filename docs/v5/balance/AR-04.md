# AR-04 balance: the Tyrant's host and the end state

Story AR-04 (design/gdd/campaign-arc.md R4, R5, R6, F2). `npm run balance` (200 seeds) ran on `v5` (5a17ad9) before the change and on the AR-04 branch after it. The new rows are printed under "Campaign arc" (`src/dev/arcBalance.ts` `hostRows`). Knob probes used scratch runs of 60–200 fights.

## What ships

- **Knobs** (`src/data/arc.ts`): `HOST` (mult, cap 32, levels, tier 3, culture share, Tyrant level 10, speed 1.2, min distance 30), `SACK_DAYS` 5, `POST_GAME` (danger +0.2, 2 reborn lairs, 10-day cooldown).
- **The host** (`src/world/world.ts`):
  - `musterHost(R)` places it at the town farthest from the start that has a path to the start, preferring one at least 30 tiles from the party. It sets the arc to `'host'` and calls `setFlag('host_musters')`. Everything about it comes from the world seed, so it draws nothing from the world Rng.
  - Size is `hostSize(R)` = `min(32, ceil(HOST.mult × R))`, where R is the fit roster at the moment it musters.
  - `hostArmy`: the Tyrant (level 10, all rare, a legendary weapon, captain and standard bearer), then two thirds men of its leaning culture and a third of the other. The lean is Greek when `arcSet` is Achilles, else Phoenician. They are mercenary lines at tier 3 fighting `hammer`, with `holdField`.
  - `thinkHost`: it marches on the starting town at 1.2× a band's roaming pace and sacks it (`isSacked`, so `isClosed` closes recruits and market for 5 days). Then it marches home and back again. A town that is still burning isn't sacked again. The march goal lives in `tx`/`ty`, so a reload resumes it.
  - Post-game: `postGame`, `danger()` (map danger +0.2, capped at 1, used by spawns, garrisons, events and event bands), `rebornSpots`, `riseReborn` and `isReborn`.
- **Campaign** (`src/game/campaign.ts`):
  - `checkArc` runs on world load and on every lair razing. It musters the host when 6 lairs have fallen. It also re-musters for a save stuck at `'host'` with no host on the map.
  - `afterPartyBattle`, host victory: `winArc` sets the arc to `'won'`, removes the host, starts the reborn clocks and puts a missing legendary set piece first in the loot (+1 pick). The Tyrant's weapon follows through the captain rule.
  - `afterPartyBattle`, host defeat: the host loses its dead (it always keeps at least 1 man) and marches on.
  - The host can't surrender. If it routs, that still counts as a victory.
  - Reborn lairs: a victory (`razeLair`) drops only a missing set piece, with no hoard and no pity. They rise again 10 days later.
  - `continueArc()` moves the arc from `'won'` to `'post'`.

The "prepared army" is my reading of the GDD: 16 Greek line men at level 7, all with tier-3 gear and every piece rare. Both sides are bots on a random campaign field, and the host holds the field.

### The tune (decision)

At the GDD's defaults the host was far too strong. Win rate follows the power plate closely, and a plate of about 1.0 gives 40–47%.

| Probe (attacker win) | Fights | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GDD default: mult 1.25, levels 8–10, tier 3 | 60 | **3%** (×1.33) |
| mult 1.0, levels 8–10 | 100 / 60 | 31% / 35% |
| mult 1.0, level 8 | 200 | 41% |
| **mult 1.0, levels 7–9 (ships)** | 200 | **49%** |
| mult 1.0, levels 7–8 | 200 | 55% |
| mult 1.25, levels 5–7 | 200 | 45% |
| mult 1.0, tier 2, levels 8–10 | 200 | 65% |

`HOST_MULT` goes to **1.0**, the bottom of the GDD's 1.0–1.5 range. That alone isn't enough (31–35%), so the seeded level window drops by one, to **7–9**.

This is the smallest deviation available: tier 3, the Tyrant and the culture mix stay as written. Keeping 1.25 would have meant levels 5–7. **Handed to the designer:** update campaign-arc.md F2 (HOST_MULT 1.0, so R = 16 gives H = 16) and R5.2 (level 7–9).

## Save safety (no bump)

The host uses existing v6 fields only: `arc.stage`, `arc.hostParty`, `arc.sackedUntil`, `PartyState.kind 'host'`, `SettlementState.reborn` and `lairs`. `tests/host.test.ts` covers:

- The v6 fixture loads at `'lairs'`, and razing its six lairs musters the host. The v5-played and v4 fixtures load at `'lairs'`.
- A save that is past the threshold (every lair fallen, arc still `'lairs'`, day 120) musters the host on load, so it is neither stuck nor skips the end.
- A save at `'host'` with no host on the map re-musters. One that has a host keeps exactly one.
- A marching host and a won arc survive a save round trip. A won save never musters again.

## Decisions (made while the user was asleep)

1. **HOST_MULT 1.0 and levels 7–9** (above).
2. **The march pace is 1.2 × a band's roaming pace (0.75).** A wandering band is the "normal band speed". The host takes about 1.4 days from the far town on the test world, so the player can intercept it.
3. **A burning town isn't re-sacked**, so it really does reopen after 5 days, as the GDD edge case says.
4. **A host defeat never kills its last man**, and the host can't surrender. A rout counts as a win. Together these mean the arc ends only with a battle won, and never by a lost host or a dead end.
5. **Reborn lairs reuse 2 of the 6 burnt lairs**, chosen by seed, rather than new map spots. Their clock is `lairs[id].lostAt`, so no new save field is needed.
6. **`'won'` and `'post'` are both post-game.** RES-01 calls `continueArc()` after the Victory screen.
7. **arcBalance.ts was edited** for the host rows, as the brief asks, even though it isn't in AR-04's ownership row.
