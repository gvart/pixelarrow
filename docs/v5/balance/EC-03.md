# EC-03 balance: the retrain cost revisit

Story EC-03 (`design/gdd/abilities.md` R7, F3). The question from the plan:
keep `RETRAIN_COST × tier` (40 × tier) after the QA-03 playtest, or change it.

**Decision: keep 40 × tier (40–200 g).** No data change. `src/data/perks.ts`,
the GDD (F3, Tuning Knobs) and every EN/RU string are unchanged ("40 g × tier",
"{n} gold per tier").

## Why keep

1. The playtester never wanted to retrain, and found the tier-1 price
   (about 1.3 early wins) fair. One proxy tester is no evidence for a change.
2. The price falls relative to income as the campaign goes on (1.3 wins at
   tier 1, 0.3 wins with resale at tier 5). That suits a forgiveness tool: a late
   mistake is cheap to undo, but an early one still costs a battle's pay.
3. 40 sits in the middle of the GDD range (20–80). The forge (EC-01) is now the
   endgame gold sink, so the retrain doesn't need to carry that job.
4. Keeping it costs no copy churn: the EN/RU strings quote "× tier".

| Alternative | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| 60 × tier | tier 1 = 2 early wins; tier 5 = 300 g, over a full tier-3 win's pay |
| A flat 40 | late retrains become trivial and the tier stops meaning anything |
| Quadratic (`40 × tier²`) | 1,000 g at tier 5 turns a forgiveness tool into a wall |

## Guard

`tests/perkPaths.test.ts`, "EC-03: stays a forgiveness price, not a wall":
`RETRAIN_COST` within the GDD's 20–80, the tier-1 price no more than one plain win
(`20 + WIN_GOLD`), and the tier-5 price no more than one tier-3 win's pay (213 g).
Watched it fail with `RETRAIN_COST = 50` (tier 5 = 250 g), then restored.
