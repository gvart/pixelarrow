The GDD's raw R3 numbers were far over budget for three actives: War-cry at −8 x1.5 morale was +35 pts, Sunder at 6 s / −3 armour / 18 s was +23, and the first Re-charge (auto charge after 2 s) was −17. They were tuned (table above). The misses are usage-bound, not magnitude-bound:
- **Bond**: F1 keeps the total, so it only delays; even a 100 s bond gave +4.
- **Aimed / Eagle Eye**: the standard army's skirmish group is 2 peltasts and a slinger, rarely 2 in range of one group. Three shots per shooter didn't help there either, and they lifted the level-4 archer mean to 71% (reverted).
- **Re-charge**: the bot already pulls riders out for free, so the active adds only the no-free-hits window and +0.4 charge. Even +3.0 charge gave +3.
- **Scythe**: one chariot, 0.8 runs a battle.

**Why the big T2 gaps stay.** Berserk and Volley stack per man: eight holders fire eight volleys (3 shots each) or rage together, while a group-wide active fires once per group. Making Volley and Rally group-wide fixed the stacking but dropped the cyclops lair from 58% to 0% (the beast fights are tuned on stacked volleys), so they keep it. An aimed volley at Volley's strength (3 shots) closed archer T2 to 58/42 but put the level-4 archer mean at 71%. The other large gaps are the R1 table's own B picks: Steady Presence or Warlord on missile T4/T5, morale-only options against damage options. Tuning one class's row moves the A path of every other class that shares the perk. That needs EC-04's class pass or a design change to R1, not a magnitude.

## Decisions (made while the user was asleep)

1. **R3 magnitudes tuned to the +5..+10 budget**: War-cry 3 morale with a 40 s cooldown, Sunder 3.6 s / −2 armour with a 30 s cooldown, Push 5.2 morale / 0.56 pace. The GDD's own figures measured +35, +23 and +2.
2. **Push doesn't move riders, chariots or myth beasts.** Militia pushes were throwing chariots back. A beast is staggered in place.
3. **Wheel and Re-charge** doesn't auto-charge after the regroup. "No free hits" means no melee blow lands on the regrouping riders. The +0.4 bonus applies to the first impact within 20 s. The bot regroups for its usual `CAV_REGROUP_S` and then picks its next charge.
4. **Aimed Volley without a focus**: the bot focuses the enemy group for the volley and clears the focus after (doctrines with `focusMissiles` prefer missile groups). Shots come from a reserve sheaf, as Volley's do. One shot per shooter (`aimedShots` knob).
5. **Group-wide actives share one cooldown per group**. Volley and Rally keep per-man cooldowns (see the beast note).
6. **Drilled** +9 morale / +6% block (A vs B mirror: hoplite T1 gap 21 → 10, thureophoros 40 → 18).
7. V5-056: bots develop path A; Volley is 2 shots; the ladder floor table is re-tuned (above).
