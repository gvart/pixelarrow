## Economy and loot: what 100 won Trials pay

Measured with the real code paths (`settleBattle`, `beastPity`, `takeLoot`), a fresh campaign (seed 77), 100 forced wins over every beast in turn (6 lair beasts, 2 world bosses), taking the best items each time. "Before" builds the fight exactly as the old `BeastTrialScene.fight` did (`beastEnemy` with its two-piece hoard, ordinary settle); "After" uses `trialBattle`.

| Mode | Won Trials | Gold | XP (sum) | Items offered | Legendary offered | Items taken | Epic taken | Legendary taken | Stash growth | Pity counter at end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before (old Trial) | 100 | 11,624 | 7,200 | 200 | 35 | 200 | 67 | 35 | 200 | 0 |
| After (Practice) | 100 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **Before:** about one legendary every 3 won Trials (35 per 100, pity included), 116 gold and 72 XP per fight, at no cost, no cooldown and no risk worth the name (a lost Trial could be closed and replayed). That is the degenerate strategy BASELINE ranked first.
- **After:** zero on every column. A Trial also costs nothing: no deaths, wounds, wear or XP (`tests/practice.test.ts` checks the whole campaign is byte-identical after 40 repeats and after a defeat).
