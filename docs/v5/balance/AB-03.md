# AB-03 balance: the Standard

`npm run balance` (200 seeds) was run on `v5` before the change (8d48d25) and on the AB-03 branch after it. Targets come from design/gdd/abilities.md ("Balance targets") and ai-doctrines.md (captain rows). Extra rows came from `standardMirror` / `standardReport` in `src/dev/classBalance.ts` and from scratch harnesses (800 seeds per arm for the knob scan; a hash-parity run over the ladder and the lairs).

## What ships (src/sim/battle.ts `STANDARD_RULES`, src/sim/stats.ts)

- **Bearer** (R5.1). `CombatStats.standard` is set by `computeStats` for the `standard_bearer` perk and for a band captain; `UnitSpec.captain` also counts. Per side: a captain first, else the highest level, ties to the lower unit id. One per army. Beasts never carry it.
- **Aura** (R5.2, F2). Radius 4 + the bearer's Will bonus, recomputed with the other auras every half second, suspended while the bearer routs. Inside: morale damage x0.85 (Steady Presence's 0.8 wins where both apply, no stacking), +0.5 morale/s out of melee, and `shockFactor` = 0.5 (V5-024: nothing else halves the cohesion shock).
- **Raise the standard** (R5.3). Once per battle, by the bearer standing (not routing or stunned). Allies within 6 regain 20% of max morale; routers left at or above 30% turn back (`rally` events). On a broken side it restores morale but turns no router back: army break wins. The human order is `{ kind: 'ability', unit: bearer, ability: 'standard' }` (`RAISE_STANDARD`); the bot raises when 3 or more allies within 6 of the bearer are routing, checked every 10 ticks (R6).
- **Banner loss** (R5.4). When the bearer is dead or knocked out, once per side: own standing men within 6 lose 12 morale, enemies within 6 gain 5 (capped at max), one `standardLost` event. The standard passes to nobody.
- **Bearer rank** (decision 1). The bearer takes slot priority 2.5 in his group: behind the front rank, ahead of the missile men.
- **Determinism.** Plain arithmetic and squared distances. The new state (bearers, raised, lost, who stands in the aura) joins `hash()` only when a side has a bearer, so every other battle hashes exactly as before. `tests/standard.test.ts` (17 tests) and `server/test/standardReplay.test.ts` (a raised and a lost standard replayed through zod to the client hash on sim 2).

## Ladder and lairs (V5-052, V5-056)

Ladder floors and lair beasts carry no standard: bots develop path A (`botPathChoice`), and `PERKS.standard_bearer` has no `standard` flag, so no bot army takes it; neither the ladder nor the lair builders mark captains. A parity run (all 50 floors × 4 seeds, each floor's bots vs their own mirror, plus hydra / cyclops / Nemean lion × 6 seeds) gives **bit-identical hashes and results** before and after. All 50 floors and the three lair rows are unchanged by construction. A player who brings a `standard_bearer` hero to the ladder gets the standard, which is the feature, not floor drift.

## Decisions (made while the user was asleep)

1. **The bearer stands behind the front rank** (slot priority 2.5, `STANDARD_RULES.rank`). In the front rank he fell in 36% of the reference mirrors and the standard was worth −1 pt; behind it 9% and +4. The GDD names no slot. It applies to captains too (shared rule), which moves the kill-captain row from −9 to −12.
2. **Raise is not an `AbilityId`.** The bearer is derived, and adding `standard` to `AbilityId` / `ABILITIES` would touch `src/data/perks.ts`, the HUD and the icon atlas (not AB-03's files). The order is the existing `ability` order with the id `'standard'` (the server zod already accepts any string ≤ 32); `Battle.canRaise`, `standardRaised`, `standardBearer` and `inStandard` are public for the HUD story. No `ability` event goes out for a raise (HUD code indexes `ABILITIES[e.ability]`); turned routers emit `rally`.
3. **Raise's 30% check is after the +20% restore**; otherwise almost no router (rout line 25%) could turn back.
4. **Banner loss is flat** (−12 / +5, no `moraleLoss`), like FO-03's shock and drain; beasts are untouched. It is detected once per tick after movement and missiles, so every death path (blow, missile, chariot crash) counts.
5. **Regen stacks with Steady Presence's regen**; only the damage reduction is "the larger applies" (F2).
6. **Ties go to the lower unit id** (spec order), not the hero id string: equal in practice and simpler.
7. **`CAPTAIN_STATS.morale` (+10) stays.** Setting it to 0 or 5 leaves the captain row at +14..+16 (400 seeds), so it isn't the lever. The captain overshoot (+15) comes from the standard on a level+2 captain who rarely falls. `CAPTAIN_RULES.levelBonus` 1 measures +12 and 0 measures +8 (400 seeds); that knob is in `src/world/world.ts` (not AB-03's), so it's **handed to the captain owner / EC-04**.
8. **Tests outside AB-03's files.** `tests/simTypes.test.ts` and `server/test/battleSchema.test.ts` asserted the captain was inert on sim 2; they now check that only for v1 (or drop the captain), because AB-03 makes him live.
