## Finding: the GDD's scenario does not exist on the map

R1.3 sizes bandits as `BANDIT_MIN + round(danger × 5) + int(0..2)` and sets the target against "a bandit band at danger 0.1–0.3". Bandits spawn at their **lair**, and `dangerAt` puts every lair at 0.35 or more by construction (the lair term alone is 0.35). On real maps it's **0.66–1.0** (240 lairs across 40 seeds: minimum 0.66, median 1.0). So a start bandit band is 6–10 men at level 2–3, and its plate reads Strong for the 8-man opening.

### BANDIT_MIN sweep against real start bandits

118 bands across 60 new worlds:

| BANDIT_MIN | Mean band | Opening wins | Plates W/We/E/S/D |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 (kept) | 8.7 | **14%** | 0/3/10/105/0 |
| 4 | 9.7 | 8% | 0/2/1/95/20 |
| 5 (GDD) | 10.7 | **3%** | 0/0/3/71/44 |
| 6 | 11.7 | 2% | 0/0/3/35/80 |

For comparison, if the danger term were dropped, bands would average about 5.7 men and the opening would win about 53%. At about 4.7 men it would win 75%. Cutting the bandits' level by 1 changes almost nothing (17% at base 3). **Size is the lever, not level.**

### Against the GDD's own scenario (danger 0.1–0.3, level 1–2)

| BANDIT_MIN | All bands | Even-plate bands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 92% | 78% |
| 6 | 82% | 68% |
| 7 | 66% | 59% |

## Decision (logged for the designer)

**BANDIT_MIN stays 3.** That's inside the GDD range of 3–6, but it isn't the GDD default of 5.

- Every new bandit band already has 5 or more men, because lair danger is at least 0.35. So AC 2 ("early bandits ≥ 5") holds, and `tests/opening.test.ts` checks it.
- Raising the base to 5 would drop the opening's first-fight win rate from 14% to 3%. It would also enlarge the bands that live players with old 3-man saves meet.
- **The 55–70% target is NOT met against real start bandits (13%).** It needs a change to the bandit curve's danger term, which is beyond R1's knobs. For example, `size = BANDIT_MIN + int(0..2)` for bandits near the start, or the danger taken away from the lair. That gives about 5–6 men and about 55–60%. **Proposed follow-up:** AR-03 owns `world.ts` next, or a dedicated tuning story.
- The 8-man opening is still a large improvement: against the same bands the old 3-man start won **0%**.
