# AR-01 balance: the 8-man opening

Story AR-01 (design/gdd/campaign-arc.md R1). Harness: `src/dev/arcBalance.ts`, printed by `npm run balance` under "Campaign arc". Both sides are bot-driven on a campaign battlefield (`campaignSetup`'s random site). Beast bands are left out.

## Result (V5-054 starter zone)

Director ruling V5-054 adds a **starter zone** to new campaigns. `World.spawnStarterBands` places 2–3 bandit bands within 5–11 tiles of the starting town, which is under 4 h of marching on day 1. The bands are independent of the lairs and come from their own seeded Rng, so the lair bands don't change. Each band has danger 0.1–0.3, size `5 + round(danger × 5) + int(0..2)` (6–9 men) and level `1 + round(danger × 2)`. Migrated and existing worlds get none.

The harness rows below come from 200 seeds and 50 new worlds:

| Row | Measured | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **8-man opening vs starter-zone bandits** (123 fights, 7.0 men, x0.92: the Even plate) | **60%** | 55–70% ✓ |
| 8-man opening vs lair bandits at the start (97 fights, 8.8 men, x1.38) | 13% | none (Strong plate) |
| Before: 3-man start vs lair bandits | 0% | reference |
| 5 volunteers (destroyed army) vs lair bandits | 0% | none stated |

## Background: the GDD's scenario did not exist on the map before V5-054

R1.3 sizes bandits as `BANDIT_MIN + round(danger × 5) + int(0..2)` and sets the target against "a bandit band at danger 0.1–0.3". Bandits spawn at their **lair**, and `dangerAt` puts every lair at 0.35 or more by construction (the lair term alone is 0.35). On real maps it's **0.66–1.0** (240 lairs across 40 seeds: minimum 0.66, median 1.0). So a start bandit band is 6–10 men at level 2–3, and its plate reads Strong for the 8-man opening.

### BANDIT_MIN sweep against real start bandits

118 bands across 60 new worlds:

| BANDIT_MIN | Mean band | Opening wins | Plates W/We/E/S/D |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 (kept) | 8.7 | **14%** | 0/3/10/105/0 |
| 4 | 9.7 | 8% | 0/2/1/95/20 |
| 5 (GDD) | 10.7 | **3%** | 0/0/3/71/44 |
| 6 | 11.7 | 2% | 0/0/3/35/80 |

For comparison, if the danger term were dropped, bands would average about 5.7 men and the opening would win about 53%. At about 4.7 men it would win 75%. Cutting the bandits' level by 1 changes almost nothing (17% at base 3). **Size is the lever, not level.**

## Lair bandits (logged for the designer)

**BANDIT_MIN stays 3 for lair bandits** (as V5-054 confirms). That's inside the GDD range of 3–6, but it isn't the GDD default of 5.

- Every new bandit band already has 5 or more men, because lair danger is at least 0.35. So AC 2 ("early bandits ≥ 5") holds, and `tests/opening.test.ts` checks it.
- Raising the base to 5 would drop the opening's first-fight win rate from 14% to 3%. It would also enlarge the bands that live players with old 3-man saves meet.
- Lair bandits stay Strong for a new army (13%). The starter zone is where the first fights happen.
- The 8-man opening is still a large improvement: against the same bands the old 3-man start won **0%**.
