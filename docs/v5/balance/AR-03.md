# AR-03 balance: tier 3, arc item sources and level pacing

Story AR-03 (design/gdd/campaign-arc.md R3, R6, R7). `npm run balance` (200 seeds) ran on `v5` (7a59c7c) before the change and on the AR-03 branch after it. The new rows are printed under "Campaign arc" (`src/dev/arcBalance.ts` `lateRows`).

## What ships

- **Tier 3** (R3, `src/world/world.ts`):
  - `TIER3_RULES = { day: 50, lairs: 2, danger: 0.85 }`, `lairTier(days, lairsFallen)` and the `World.lairsFallen` / `World.lairTier` getters.
  - `tier3Band`: while the lair tier is 3, a new band forming where danger is over 0.85 is gear tier 3; once 2 lairs have fallen, so is every raider and mercenary band. It applies to lair and town spawns and to event bands. Starter-zone bands are untouched.
  - The rule draws nothing from the world Rng, so a world spawns the same bands (men, seeds, places) as before. Only their tier changes.
  - `BAND_CAPS` go from 18 / 8 to **22 / 10**.
- **Arc item sources** (R6, `src/game/sources.ts`):
  - `lairHoard(seed, culture, ids, classes)`: 3 pieces of tier-2-and-up gear. The first is a sure epic, and 25% of the time it's a piece of the lair culture's epic set (`LAIR_SET`: Greek Sacred Band, Phoenician Immortals, Celtic Brennus). The others roll `rollRarity(tier 3)`.
  - `Campaign.lairHoard(lairId)`: seeded by world seed + lair id, follows the army, and counts on the campaign pity counter (`data.pity`).
  - `arcSet(worldSeed)`: Achilles or Alexander, fixed per world, about 50/50.
  - `arcSetPiece(set, owned, seed)`: a missing piece, or null once all five are owned.
- **Level pacing** (R7, `src/game/loot.ts`): `LATE_XP = { from: 6, mult: 1.25 }`. A hero at level 6 or higher gains 1.25× campaign battle XP. That's the GDD's `XP_CURVE_L6PLUS = 32·L` without touching `xpToNext` (`src/data/units.ts`, owned by EC-04), so XP bars stay correct.

## Economy

| Row (all men killed, the 4 dearest pieces at corpse wear) | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loot resale per win | 310 g | **479 g (+55%)** |
| Battle pay per win (`20 + 12·kills + 60`) | 213 g | 213 g |
| Lair hoard resale (3 picks, one time, 6 lairs) | - | 296 g a chest |

This rule (all men killed, the 4 dearest pieces) is generous next to BASELINE's 120–200 g a win. The ratio is what matters: **loot resale still beats battle pay, and at tier 3 by more** (2.2× against 1.5×). Late-game gold rises from day 50, and the campaign has no endgame sink. This is the GDD's known gap, the forge reroll, and it goes to **EC-01** (which depends on AR-03). Rarity tables and `bandSet` (4%) are unchanged, as the GDD asks.

## Save safety

There's no save bump and no migration. `WorldSave.lairs` already exists in v6 (empty for every live save), so `lairsFallen` is 0 until AR-02 lets lairs fall. Tier is an existing `PartyState` number, so bands already on the map keep their tier. A test loads the v6 fixture and checks that the parties are byte-identical. A live save past day 50 starts getting tier-3 spawns at hot spots from its next spawn. The XP multiplier is applied when XP is awarded and isn't stored.

## Decisions (logged for the designer)

1. **Day 50 gates the danger rule.** The GDD's "tier 3 when danger > 0.85" applies only while the lair tier is 3. Without the gate, most lair bands (danger 0.66–1.0) would be tier 3 from day 0. That contradicts the AC and W10 ("tier 3 arrives too early").
2. **R7 adopted as an XP multiplier,** 1.25× from level 6, rather than editing `xpToNext`, which EC-04 owns this wave. The result is the same as 32·L for any gain that doesn't cross level 5→6.
3. **The R6 sources ship as functions.** `Campaign.lairHoard`, `arcSet` and `arcSetPiece` are tested but not yet called. AR-02 (lair victory) and AR-04 (host, reborn lairs) wire them. Constants live in `world.ts` / `sources.ts` until AR-02 creates `src/data/arc.ts`.
4. **The band caps rose as the GDD says.** Natural band sizes peak at 16, so the 22-man cap only binds for event bands. The level cap 10 binds from about day 80 for raiders and mercenaries.
