# FW-01 balance: field works in the sim

`npm run balance` (200 seeds) was run on `v5` before the change (9716188) and on the FW-01 branch after it. Targets come from design/gdd/field-works.md ("Balance targets"). The works rows come from `worksRows` in `src/dev/worksBalance.ts`. Extra rows came from scratch harnesses: a placement sweep for the flank row (300 seeds per arm), a knob scan for the ditch row (120 seeds per arm), and a hash-parity run over the ladder, the lairs and terrain battles.

## What ships

- **Cell kinds** (R1, `src/data/terrain.ts`). `stakes` x, `ditch` d, `laager` L, `palisade` P and `rampart` p use the R1 multipliers. They are appended after the old kinds, so every old grid keeps its cell indices. The `WORKS` table holds the knobs: segment lengths 3/3/2, the allowed ground, DITCH_MULT 1.2, IMPALE_MULT 0.5, costs 6/8/15, max 3, caught 1, GARRISON_STAKES 2. The sim reads only the first four. FW-02 and AR-02 read the rest.
- **`work` / `unwork`** (R3, `src/sim/battle.ts`). A segment is centred on the cell under (x, y) and laid across (fx, fy). The facing snaps to the nearest of the 8 grid directions. An order is refused when it comes outside deploy (`phase`), when the side has no allowance left for that type or already has 3 segments (`allowance`), when it falls off the grid (`bounds`), when a cell lies outside the side's deployment zone (`zone`), when it overlaps another work (`occupied`), or when the ground isn't open, scrub, sand or rough (`ground`). A refused order is ignored, recorded in `Battle.worksLog` (output only), and still kept in `orderLog`, so a replay refuses it the same way. `unwork` restores the ground and frees the allowance. A field without a grid gets an open, flat one when the first work is placed. `Battle.workRefusal()` (for the deployment ghost's ✗ reason) and `Battle.works()` are public.
- **Ditch** (R2.1, F1). In `meleeAttack`, beside the height factor: `max(heightFactor, 1.2)` when the target stands in a ditch and the striker doesn't.
- **Stakes** (R2.2, F2). Checked once per tick after movement and separation. A man entering a stakes cell loses his momentum and keeps none while he stays in it. If he was charging, one `stakesBreak` fires per crossing, at the cell's centre. A rider entering takes `0.5 × dmg × (1.4 + chargeBonus × 0.6 × speed ratio)` through armour, credited to nobody, and is pulled up to the horse's pace in stakes (0.3, or 0.2 for a chariot). Beasts treat stakes as rough ground, with no reset and no impale. Myth bosses walk through works.
- **Determinism.** The works state (each segment's side, type and cells, plus who stands in stakes) joins `hash()` only once stakes or a ditch are on the field, or a work has been placed. Every other battle plays and hashes as before. `tests/works.test.ts` has 18 tests. `server/test/worksReplay.test.ts` replays, through zod, a battle with stakes, a ditch, a refused order and an unwork, and a second battle whose charge breaks on the stakes, to the client hash on sim 2. No schema change was needed: ST-03 had already declared the kinds.

## No-works hash parity (ladder, lairs, online)

A scratch harness hashed 291 battles on `v5` and on this branch and found them **bit-identical**:

- all 50 ladder floors × 4 seeds, through `ladderSetup`, with the floor's bot against the player's bot-driven mirror;
- hydra, cyclops and Nemean lion × 6 seeds (`runBeast`);
- 20 matched battles;
- 10 flank battles;
- 25 battles on generated terrain (every site base, with rivers, coasts and rocks).

So all 50 floors and the three lair rows are unchanged by construction (V5-052, V5-056). Online play sends no works (V5-022: allowance 0), so live replays take the unchanged path.

## Decisions (made while the user was asleep)

1. **(x, y) of `work` / `unwork` are field coordinates**, mapped to the cell under them. The renderer and the drag tools already work in field coordinates. The segment runs along the facing's right, snapped to 8 directions. A 2-cell laager runs from the centre cell to its right.
2. **Ditch factor in `meleeAttack`**, where the height factor is computed, not in `applyDamage` (which also takes missiles, scythes and the balk counter). F1 speaks of melee blows only.
3. **Impale is a direct hit credited to nobody** (like a chariot crash). It emits no `hit` event, because the HUD indexes `units[by]`. The F2 blow uses the rider's speed ratio at entry (clamped 0.4–1.15, as in the charge), so a walking horse is impaled too (R2.2: "Mounted units entering").
4. **"Crossing" is judged by the man's centre cell** at the end of each tick. A man deployed on stakes hasn't crossed them.
5. **Charge reset holds while he stays in stakes**, so a foot charge can't rebuild inside a 1-pace row. A rider is also capped to the stakes pace, because his momentum is recomputed from his speed every tick.
6. **Lance reach vs stakes distance (input for FW-02 / AR-02).** A Companion strikes from about 3 paces, so riders charging a line head on hit it over a stakes row laid 2 paces out without entering it. The harness row lays the row 3.5 paces out. R6's "2 paces out" for garrison stakes should be re-measured by AR-02. It still works against riders sweeping a flank.
7. **Allowance is clamped per type to WORKS_MAX, and a side holds at most WORKS_MAX segments in all**, whatever `ArmySpec.works` says.
8. **Tests outside FW-01's files.** `tests/simTypes.test.ts` asserted that `worksRows` was empty until FW landed. That assertion is removed, and the rows are covered here. `server/test/worksReplay.test.ts` is new.
