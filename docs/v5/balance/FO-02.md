# FO-02 balance: orb and feint

`npm run balance` (200 seeds), run on `v5` before the change (45be15b) and on the FO-02 branch after it, then again after V5-041. Targets are from design/gdd/formations-orders.md "Balance targets".

## What changed in the sim

- **Orb** (`FormationType 'orb'`, R2). The men stand in a ring centred on the formation anchor and face outward. The ring is two deep above 24 men. The radius is `max(1.2, n × 0.12)`, with n = ceil(n/2) when two deep. When an orb man falls or breaks, the ring re-slots at once on the smaller radius, closing the threatened arc (V5-041). A blow or missile from outside the ring lands on the front. An attacker inside the ring uses the normal rule. The costs are speed ×0.4 and missile accuracy ×0.7 (V5-041 dropped the ×0.85 damage). There is no shield wall. A `charge` opens the orb into a line at its facing first. Bracing works: a charge breaks on a braced orb, horses shy from spears and spearmen don't dread horses. Riders can't form an orb: `preset` is a no-op, `form` keeps the shape, and a setup orb with riders, or a rider assigned into an orb, stands in line. The ring slots use Taylor-series cos/sin and a pseudo-angle, so no `Math.cos` or `atan2` is used (ADR-0001).
- **Feint** (`order: 'feint'`, R3). Eligible means every man standing is `light` or `ranged` by class, or a rider. Such a group runs back at 1.4× its walk for 4 s, facing away. It then turns about and gets `charge`, and each man's first blow does +30% damage. An enemy group pursues if it has an advance or charge order, the feinting group is its nearest enemy group, and it has moved more than 3 paces toward it. At the turn-about, a pursuing group is disordered for 3 s: its block drops by 0.15, its slots scatter by up to ±0.3 paces, and the sim emits one `disordered` event. The cooldown is 30 s per group. The order is a no-op if the group is ineligible, routed, cooling down or still in deployment; the order log keeps the entry. Any other order cancels a running feint.

## Decisions (made while the user was away)

1. The ring is centred on the formation anchor, so `face` and the switch from line to orb pivot in place. A moving orb turns at once, with no wheel ("the orb doesn't turn" — there is no front to wheel).
2. A blow is "outside the ring" when the attacker's distance from the centre is greater than the outer radius.
3. Bracing in an orb counts wherever the shield-wall brace counts (`braced`, the horse shy and the horse-dread immunity), but the orb gets no +0.15 shield-wall block.
4. Feint eligibility uses the man's `stats.cls` looked up in `CLASSES` (an own-property check, so a hostile `cls` like `__proto__` is just unknown). A man with no known class counts by his stats: a rider, or a missile or javelin man.
5. "Logged" for an ineligible feint means it stays in `orderLog`, which is what the server replays and stores. The sim never writes to the console.
6. "Its attack target" in R3.3: `SimGroup` has no attack-target field, so the pursuit test uses the nearest enemy group by centroid only.
7. "Full speed" is 1.4× walk, the run men use to catch up their slots. It doesn't build charge momentum, and it has no stamina surcharge.
8. "−15% block" is an absolute −0.15 on the block chance, mirroring the shield wall's +0.15.
9. The first-blow bonus (+0.3 charge bonus) is ×1.3 damage on each man's next melee blow, whether or not he has charge momentum.
10. Disorder starts at the turn-about, when the trap springs, not at the moment of pursuit.
11. Feint state, disorder and the orb radius are private Battle fields. They're not added to `SimGroup` (types.ts is ST-03's) and not hashed: they act only through positions, HP and morale, which are hashed.

## Online and server

- zod derives its shape enum from `FORMATION_TYPES`, so `orb` entered `OrderSchema` (`form`, `preset`) and the setup's group formations with the sim rule. `feint` was already in `GROUP_ORDERS`. Malformed shapes (`circle`, `ORB`, `__proto__`) are refused by zod. The sim also ignores an unknown shape in an order, and a setup with one stands in line.
- The frozen v1 sim throws on an orb (an old in-flight ticket whose client sends one). The duel, async and attack verify paths catch it as **422 `sim_rejected`**, and so does `/api/battle/verify`. Tested in `server/test/orbFeintReplay.test.ts`. Live sockets need sim 2 (floors raised), so a v1 relay can't receive an orb.
- `server/test/orbFeintReplay.test.ts`: the server replays a client log with an orb in the setup and an orb in deployment, two feints, an ineligible feint and a charge out of an orb, through zod, to the client hash, on three seeds.
