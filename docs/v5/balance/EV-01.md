# EV-01 balance: the overland event engine

Story EV-01 (design/gdd/campaign-events.md R1-R4, F1). The engine ships with an **empty deck** (`EVENT_DECK = []`); the content and its EV table are EV-02's (`src/dev/eventBalance.ts`). EV-01 defines no balance rows.

## `npm run balance`

Run on v5 at 9716188 before the story and again on the branch after it (200 matched battles). The two reports are **identical** except for the run time. With the deck empty, no event is drawn, and an empty deck consumes no randomness, so band movement and spawns are unchanged too (tested in `tests/events.test.ts`: "the shipped deck is EV-02 content").

The refactor of `spawnParty` into `bandShape` keeps the same draws in the same order. The arc harness rows (`arcBalance`, which spawns bands) match to the digit.

## Engine rates (from the test deck)

- A march check comes each time the clock passes a multiple of 12 game hours while the party travels. With no cap or gap, the measured hit rate over 100 days of marching was within 0.05-0.16 of the checks (the knob is 0.10). Across 199 worlds × 100 slots, the seeded roll hit 9.8%.
- The pacing cap (1 per game day) and the 24 h gap hold in a scripted march (no two events on the same day or less than 24 h apart).
- After a won band battle, the `after_battle` roll fires in 3-15 of 30 worlds, as the test asserts (the knob is 0.25).

F1's 0.3-0.5 events per battle depends on the deck's eligibility and cooldowns, so EV-02's scripted 40-battle run measures it.

## Decisions (autonomous; the user was asleep)

1. **Seeded rolls per check, not the shared world RNG.** Each check rolls `Rng(hash(worldSeed, trigger, slot))`. The slot is the 12 h march slot, the camp night, the day for flag events, the settlement and arrival time, or the battle count. `WorldSave.rng` also drives band AI, and `WorldScene` advances it by frame-time-sized steps. Drawing events from it would make the draw depend on frame timing. The hashed roll is deterministic per world seed and action (AC 1), even across different step sizes (tested), and it leaves band behaviour untouched.
2. **No new save fields.** Engine bookkeeping rides the v6 maps under reserved `@` keys: `eventCd['@gap']` (the end of the 24 h gap), `eventCd['@arrive:<id>']` (the arrival cooldown), `flags['@next:<bonus>']` (pending nextBattle bonuses), `flags['@retrain']` (free retrains owed), and `flags`/`eventCd['@price:<id>']` and `eventCd['@closed:<id>']` (settlement mods). Expired cooldowns are pruned at each draw.
3. **Optional PartyState fields** `hunts`, `bountyMult` and `doctrine`, read with defaults (ADR-0002, "a field found later must be optional"). `bountyMultOf` clamps to 1..3 and reads a malformed value as 1. `save.ts` (not EV-01's file) does not repair them; the readers are defensive instead.
4. **A flag event's delay** is an `after: { flag, days: [min, max] }` clause on the EventDef. The flag's value is the day it was set, and the delay is seeded per world. This is the GDD's `flag:<name>@day`. The arc's `host_musters` is `World.setFlag('host_musters')` with `days: [0, 0]`.
5. **Two condition and effect extensions** are needed by the GDD's own deck: `cond.escaped` (`rival_returns`) and the place `'here'` for priceMod/closeSettlement. `item(rarity, slot?)` rolls a tier ≤ 2 base piece. Set pieces by culture are left for EV-02 if the deck needs them.
6. **nextBattle bonuses are spent by the next band battle** (every campaign battle is one), whatever its result. Gold is +50% of the battle's gold after the goldBonus cap and the retreat cut, applied before the bounty.
7. **A pending id the deck no longer holds is dropped** on read, so a content change can't freeze the map.

## Not built (known gaps)

- A chained event whose band was already killed doesn't clear its flag (GDD edge case). This needs a band-to-flag link; it's left for EV-02 or EV-03 if a chain needs it.
- A `ford_toll` "near a river" condition doesn't exist.
