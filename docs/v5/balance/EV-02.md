# EV-02 balance: the starter event deck

Story EV-02 (design/gdd/campaign-events.md R5, R6, F2, "Balance targets"). The deck is 24 events in `src/data/eventDeck.ts`, with card text in `src/i18n/events.en.ts` and `events.ru.ts`. The EV table and the economy run are in `src/dev/eventBalance.ts`.

## The flag (V5-064, V5-070)

The deck ships **off**. `EVENT_DECK_ENABLED = false`, so `liveDeck()` returns `[]`. `World.deck` still defaults to EV-01's empty `EVENT_DECK`, and nothing imports the new modules except the tests. With the flag off, live players see no events and nothing in the world changes. `tests/eventDeck.test.ts` checks this: a world given `liveDeck()` marches 400 steps exactly like one with no deck, never has a pending event, and after-battle checks return null.

**EV-03 enables the deck in two lines.** It sets `EVENT_DECK_ENABLED = true` and points `World.deck` at `liveDeck()` from `src/world/world.ts`. It must not import from `events.ts`: `eventDeck.ts` imports `fx` from `events.ts`, so that import would be a cycle that fails at load. EV-03 also spreads `EVENTS_EN` and `EVENTS_RU` into `en.ts` and `ru.ts` for `t()`. Neither of those files is EV-02's.

## Targets (flag ON, 200 worlds × 40 battles)

The script plays F1's typical day through the real engine (`World.tryEvent`: rates, the daily cap, the 24 h gap, cooldowns and flags) and the real resolution (`Campaign.chooseEvent`):

- 10 march hours a day;
- 0.7 arrivals a day, half at towns and half at villages, with the 3-day arrival cooldown;
- 0.3 camp nights a day;
- 1.2 battles a day, with 1.0 wins a day.

Each win pays BASELINE's 105 g into the purse, so that costs are payable. This gold isn't counted as event gold. Spawned bands are valued with F2 and removed. The roster is trimmed to 12 men after each battle.

The player is modelled two ways:

- **random**: a uniform pick among the choices he can take, an average player;
- **greedy**: always the best EV.

Draws by trigger (random policy, 200 worlds) were:

Under the random policy the pick shares are uniform by design. The highest observed share, 61% (thief_relapse:flog, 25 of 41 picks), is sampling noise. The GDD's "no choice above 80%" is a playtest check of real players and can't be measured headlessly.

**Gold, XP and risk.** The GDD sets no target for these, so they aren't judged:

- Gold per day is −0.7 g (random) and −3.4 g (greedy). A battle pays about 105 g, so events are a mild sink, as E2 wants: costs, tolls and bribes outweigh the windfalls.
- XP per day is 3.0 (random) and 9.5 (greedy), against about 240 for a won battle.
- Events send 0.02–0.03 extra fights per battle, for 0.04–0.05 F2 deaths per battle.
- Events add 0.4–0.6 wound-hours per battle.

Events shape a campaign, but they don't fund it.

## The EV table (F2)

The GDD fixes these weights: 1 gold = 1, 1 food = 2, 1 man = 60, 1 wound-hour = −1. It gives no weight for the other parts. These are assumed (`EV_ASSUME`):

- 1 XP on one man = 0.5 g. A level-1 man needs 40 XP to level up, worth about 20 g of a town hire's premium.
- `all` targets 8 men, the opening army.
- `heal` clears 50 wound-hours (BASELINE: 1.4 wounds × 36 h).
- An uncommon item is worth 48.7 g: the mean tier 1–2 base value × 1.12.
- `nextBattle`: `xp` = 60 g, `gold` = 52.5 g, `pick` = one common item at 43.5 g.
- Revealing a lair = 15 g, a radius = 10 g, and one garrison man = 10 g.
- A retrain = 40 g, the drillmaster's tier-1 price.
- One price % = 1 g, for one hire of about 100 g in the window.
- A spawned band at power ratio r has p_win = 0.5 − 1.25(r − 1) and 2r deaths, with BASELINE's 2 deaths in an even fight. A captain adds 0.15 to r, and his bounty is `captainBounty(4, bountyMult)`.

`chain` is the best EV of the follow-up event that the flag makes due. The GDD values a flag at 0 and lists the follow-up separately. That separate column is `chain`, and it is what keeps "Take them in" from dominating "Turn them away". `risk` is minus the chance that a choice goes badly. It is a dimension of the dominance test, not part of the EV sum.

## Decisions (autonomous; the user was asleep)

1. **The gate is a constant plus `liveDeck(enabled)`.** No engine file is edited (`events.ts`, `world.ts` and `en.ts` belong to other stories). EV-03's switch-on is described above.
2. **The oracle is dormant** (weight 0, behind `RETRAIN_SPENDABLE = false`). Its paid choice is the GDD's free retrain. `Campaign.useFreeRetrain` exists, but no screen spends it yet (the drillmaster is SET-01). A card must not sell a feature that doesn't exist. Its "Leave" choice was dropped, because the free "Ask about the lairs" dominated it.
3. **`deserters_hunt` "Hand them over" became "Pay his price" (−60 g).** The closed vocabulary has no effect that removes men.
4. **`caravan` pays in advance** (+80 g and an uncommon item). The raiders are the chained `caravan_raid` 1–2 days later (fight a hunting raider band, or leave 50 g). The engine can't make a reward depend on winning a fight.
5. **`rival_returns` sends a fresh hunting mercenary captain.** The vocabulary can't name the band that escaped. `rival_beaten` ("boosts later recruit offers") isn't built, because no effect can see who won a fight.
6. **`ford_toll` has no river condition**, which EV-01 doesn't have. The text says "the only crossing".
7. **The `deserter_map` garrison cut is silent.** `weakenGarrison` is stored, but lair assaults (AR-02) don't read it yet, so the card promises only the map.
8. **`host_musters`** is the GDD's one-choice narrative card. It can't fire until the arc sets the flag (AR-02 and AR-04).
9. **12 new events**, all built from existing features: wounded_foe → officer_repays, battlefield_spoils, mercenary_offer, camp_fever, thieves → thief_relapse, harvest → village_warning, bandit_scouts → bandits_vengeance, and caravan_raid. Stripping the dead also sets `ares_angry`, so two events feed `ares_wrath`. That makes 8 of 24 events chain setters.
10. **The text carries no numbers.** The card's chips show costs and effects from the data, so the text can't drift from them (this is tested). Each choice has a result line (`r.`), and each chance choice also has a failure line (`f.`), for the card and the journal.
