`npm run balance` (200 matched seeds; the doctrine and captain rows run 400 seeds each), before and after AI-02. Targets are from design/gdd/ai-doctrines.md "Balance targets" and F3, and the gold target in design/gdd/campaign-arc.md.

## Existing rows: before → after

**Every row printed before AI-02 prints the same number after it**: matched 54% / 85.0 s / 11.0 s, passive 7%, ridge 73%, the perk, terrain, class, beast, rarity, doctrine and orders rows. No harness army has a captain, and a hero without the captain's mark derives exactly the stats he did before. The run takes 321 s instead of 117 s, because the captain rows add 1,600 battles.

## Tuning done

- **No stand-in standard aura.** The first build gave the captain Steady Presence as a stand-in for the Standard (abilities.md R5). That made him worth **+23 points** (B3/P3, 300 seeds: the reference won 58% against the plain band and 35% against the captained band), against a +5..+10 target. Without the aura he is worth +9 to +10. The Standard is a sim rule (aura radius, Raise, banner loss) that belongs to AB-03 in `src/sim/battle.ts`, which reads `UnitSpec.captain`. So a captain now carries only his level (+2), his gear floor and `CAPTAIN_STATS.morale` (+10).
- **The operating point.** The reference won 98% against the band at band level 3 and player level 4. Both at level 3 puts it near 50%, so the rows can show movement in either direction.

## Why the kill-captain and bounty-EV rows miss

1. **No order targets a man.** `focus` aims at a group's nearest man, and `charge` goes for the nearest enemy, so a strike team can't single the captain out. The script detaches the swordsman and both peltasts at first contact and sends them at him every half second, with `charge`, `hold` and `advance` all tried. The captain still fell in only 7–9% of battles, and taking three men out of the line costs more than it gains. Against a control that sends the same team at another man, killing the captain is worth +5 points. That is his real value today.
2. **His fall costs his band little.** Without banner loss (abilities.md R5.4: −12 morale within 6 paces), losing the captain is losing one good man. AB-03 brings banner loss. A tap-to-attack on a single unit (ai-doctrines.md "Touch UI needs", CT stories) would make him a reachable target.
3. **So the bounty is rare.** It is paid in about 9% of wins (33 of 374), which spreads to +3% of win gold instead of +10..+20%. When it is paid, it is 31% of that win's gold, in band.

Re-measure both rows after AB-03 lands and after the player can target a unit. If the EV is still short, the levers are: count a captain caught routing on a won field as captured, or raise `bountyMult` for captained bands.

## Not built here

- **Counter-script rows** (rush ← brace, hammer ← reserve faced to the flank, and the others): no story owns them, and the harness doesn't have them.
- **ESCAPE_CAP's "fights to the end"**: `escaped` counts to 3, but turning `retreatAt` to 0 at 3 needs a per-army field in the sim (ai.ts / types.ts).
