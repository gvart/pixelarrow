# EC-02 balance: the item power cap

Story EC-02 (GDD_REVIEW N16: "gear power drifts toward stats decide"; keep item deltas at or under 10 pts). `npm run balance` (200 seeds) ran on `v5` (ea8321c) before the change and on the EC-02 branch after it. The item rows were also run at 400 seeds per cell with the same harness (`itemDeltas` in `src/dev/rarityBalance.ts`), because the default run's 120 seeds leave a 4.6-point standard error.

## The cap

- **Definition** (new; nothing in the GDDs said what an "item delta" is): the win rate, minus 50, of the rarity mirror's reference army with the item fitted to every man whose class may use it, against the same army without it. Level 5 and level 10. A set is judged by its bonus lines (both sides wear the pieces; side 1 has the bonus off). An item the reference army can't use replaces its last four men with the class that wears it most.
- **Cap:** +10 points. A row prints OVER when it is more than 1 SE above (50/√n). Rule and table: docs/ITEMS.md "Item power cap".
- **Harness:** `itemCapCases`, `runItemCap`, `itemDeltas`, `itemCapReport`, `ITEM_DELTA_CAP` in `src/dev/rarityBalance.ts`. `rarityReport` prints the cap rows under the rarity mirror, so `npm run balance` shows both (it adds about 170 s to the 200-seed run: 519 s → 687 s).

## Decisions (logged here; the integrator may copy them to DECISIONS.md)

1. **What an item delta is.** The rarity mirror with one item on every man who can use it, at level 5 and 10, 1 SE tolerance. It matches the existing mirror's scale (a full legendary kit is about +37, five slots about +8 each).
2. **Re-tune data, don't clamp at runtime.** Stats are already computed from data at use time, so trimming the data is the cap; a runtime clamp would have hidden which item is too strong.
3. **Plain legendary weapon and shield accepted** at about +11.5 (1.5 points over, about one SE at 400 seeds). They are the reference; the legendary kit is on its 85% target; trimming them would weaken every legendary weapon and shield players own.
4. **missileWard and shroud kept** (0.4, 0.3): trimming the Helm of Hades' shroud to 0.2 didn't move its row; its fixed stats and armour did.
