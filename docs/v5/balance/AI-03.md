# AI-03 balance: ladder and war doctrines

`npm run balance` (200 matched seeds) was run before and after AI-03. The ladder and war rows below came from a throwaway harness (400 seeds per row, not committed). Targets: design/gdd/ai-doctrines.md R4, amended by the Director's ruling **V5-052**: doctrines must not lower ladder difficulty or Glory, and every ladder floor must stay within ±5 pts of its pre-AI-03 rate.

## Existing rows: before → after

**Identical.** The reports differ only in wall time. AI-03 changes only server-built setups and adds `ArmySpec.holdField`, which nothing in the harness sets.

## What ships

| Source | Doctrine |
| --- | --- |
| Ladder floors 1–9 | steady |
| Ladder floors 11–49 (not bosses) | the chapter's doctrine (`CHAPTER_DOCTRINE`: 2 swarm, 3 rush, 4 hammer, 5 fury), unless `FLOOR_DOCTRINE` in `src/duel/ladder.ts` overrides it |
| Ladder bosses 10–50 | **steady** (V5-052 point 2). The GDD's culture rule made floor 10 a rush boss, and its bot's win rate against its mirror fell from 29% to 15%. |
| Every ladder floor | `holdField: true`: the bot **never retreats**, whatever its doctrine (V5-052 point 1) |
| War neutrals | by defender kind (`src/online/neutralDoctrine.ts`): hill tribes and pirates swarm, cultists fury, the rest steady. Retreat stays on. |
| Lair beasts, militia, player garrisons, world bosses | steady |

### Why a per-floor table

Even with retreat off, a chapter's doctrine swung single floors by up to ±30 pts against their steady mirror. The swing depends on the floor's mix (for example, swarm at floor 12 was −27 and fury at floor 42 was +18). So every floor 11–49 was scanned against all four doctrines at 400 seeds:

1. The floor keeps its chapter doctrine if that stays within ±5.
2. Otherwise it gets the passing doctrine closest to 0.
3. If none passes, it plays steady.

Result: of the 36 floors from 11 to 49 that are not bosses, 16 keep their chapter doctrine, 15 get another doctrine, and 5 play steady (14, 31, 42, 46 and 48), as do the bosses. Every chapter keeps its own doctrine on at least 2 floors; fury, in chapter 5, keeps only 43 and 47.

**Re-measure this table after any sim or AI change** (AB-02, ST-04). It was tuned against the sim as it is at this commit.

## War neutrals, tier 3, vs a steady mirror (400 seeds)

| Row | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tribes (swarm) | 48% / 71 s / 0% | 43% / 45 s / **54% retreat** |
| pirates (swarm) | 27% / 79 s / 0% | 34% / 63 s / **66% retreat** |
| cultists (fury) | 47% / 45 s / 0% | 48% / 36 s / 0% |

**Reward when neutrals retreat** (V5-052 point 3): a bot retreat ends the battle with the attacker as `winner`. `resolveAttack` → `resolveBattle` therefore pays a full victory: the 60 g win bonus, a siege win toward the claim, and loot picks under the victory rules. `retreated` only marks the player's own retreat. What shrinks is the per-kill part: neutrals who fled are not killed, so they add no 12 g kill gold and no items to the loot pool (`lootPool` only strips the dead). Steady, fury and the other neutrals never retreat.

## Compatibility

- Doctrines are derived from the floor or region and never stored, so no migration.
- `holdField` is optional, and missing means false. Server zod accepts it as a boolean.
- A ticket issued before AI-03 has neither field and replays as before. An in-flight v1 ticket still verifies with the frozen v1 sim, which ignores both fields (server/test/neutrals.test.ts).
- The async defence doctrine picker is not built. It is already in the plan's cut list (SPRINTS.md, ANSWERS A-37).
- No doctrine prefers the orb (V5-041), and a test checks this.
