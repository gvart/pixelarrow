# AB-01 balance: perk paths A/B

`npm run balance` (200 seeds) was run on `v5` before the change (4dea4d3) and on the AB-01 branch after it. Targets come from design/gdd/abilities.md, "Balance targets". An extra A-vs-B harness (described below) measured the one AB-01 target that `npm run balance` doesn't cover.

## What changed

- **Paths.** `ClassDef.tree` is now five `[A, B]` tiers (`PerkPath[]`). A is the old linear perk. B comes from the GDD R1 family table. The full per-class table is in `src/data/classes.ts`, and `tests/perkPaths.test.ts` checks that every class has 10 distinct perks.
- **`perkBlocker`** (shared by the campaign, the duel client and the duel server's `/develop` through `developHero`) accepts A or B at a tier. It refuses a second pick in a tier with "Path chosen", which is checked first because it's the most lasting reason. A tier needs **any** perk of the tier before it, plus the level and a free point.
- **New ids** are data only: `push`, `bond`, `warcry`, `sunder`, `aimed`, `recharge`, `scythe` and `standard_bearer`. AB-02 gives the seven their `ability`, and AB-03 sets `standard` and the aura. Until then they do nothing in battle.
- **`autoDevelop`** (R6) is deterministic. A bot takes B at tier 2 only when that B grants an ability, and `standard_bearer` only when it carries the standard (`PerkDef.standard`) and the army has none. `makeHero` reads its roster to check this, and `setBotLevel` and `autoDevelop` take an optional `{ standard }` context. Today neither B is live, so **every bot army, ladder floor and war neutral is unchanged**. They switch over automatically when AB-02 and AB-03 wire the effects.
- **Retrain** (R7, F3): `RETRAIN_COST = 40`, `retrainCost(tier)` = 40 × tier (40–200), and `retrainPerks(h, tier)` swaps one tier to its other option in place.
- **Stats** (`src/sim/stats.ts`): a perk's `range` now applies only to a man with a missile weapon. Before, path B's Longshot on a spear-armed peltast made him a "ranged" unit with no ammunition. No A path is affected, because every A range perk sits on a class that always carries missiles.

## Migration of existing perks (G-SAVE, no new bump)

- **Rule.** Old perk at chain position *i* = option **A** of tier *i*. `heroTree()` still returns the A path, so the old chain is exactly path A.
- **Campaign saves.** The data doesn't change. Save v6's validation keeps every id in `PERKS`, so B ids round-trip. A pre-v6 client refuses a v6 save ("newer"), so it can't drop B ids.
- **Duel D1 heroes.** The data doesn't change, and no migration is needed. Stored linear chains read as all-A and keep developing. In-flight ladder tickets replay their stored setup.
- **Classless heroes (very old saves).** They keep the legacy linear trees: one option per tier, and their mixed tier-0 picks are still allowed. Nothing a player has is refused or removed.
- **Tests.** `tests/perkPaths.test.ts` covers a real v5 fixture with full old chains (all A), a stored D1 archer, and B ids surviving a save. `server/test/perks.test.ts` covers `/develop`, an old D1 row, the budget, and a ladder replay containing a B pick.

## A vs B per tier (AC 3, target |A − B| ≤ 6 pts)

**Harness.** Scratch code, not committed, built on `classSquad`. Each side has 8 men of one class at level 10 on path A. Side B swaps one tier to B. Both sides are bot-driven, sides alternate, and each row uses 100 seeds. **Control** (identical sides): militia 48/52 and archer 44/56. That puts the noise floor of this head-to-head at about ±10 pts. Rows whose B isn't live yet (T2 signature actives and `standard_bearer`) are measured in AB-02 and AB-03: **29 rows**.

| Result | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| Met (≤ 6) | 12 |
| Within the noise floor (7–12) | 12 |
| Clear miss (13–30) | 11 |
| Large miss (> 30) | 26 |

**Largest gaps** (A win% vs B win%):

| Gap | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| 100 / 0 | archer T4 eagle_eye vs steady_presence; archer T5 skirmish_master vs warlord |
| 95–94 / 5–6 | slinger T1, T4, T5 (A wins) |
| 19 / 81 | thureophoros T3 (B wins) |
| 18 / 79 | chariot T4 ride_down vs rally_cry (B wins) |
| 24 / 76 | falx T3 (B wins) |

**Pattern.** Missile auras and damage beat morale perks in an all-missile mirror. Morale-only options (drilled, iron_discipline, zealot) lose to damage or armour options in pure-class mirrors. A same-class mirror amplifies a small edge into a rout, so it's far harsher than the `perkImpact` mixed-army mirror, where every perk is worth 0–5 pts (BASELINE).

**Verdict: MISS (accepted for now, logged as an AB-01 decision).** Fixing this needs magnitude changes to shared perks (Eagle Eye, Warlord, Volley and the morale perks). Those change the A paths of other classes, and R2's re-tune of those perks belongs to AB-02. The rows to re-check after AB-02 and EC-04 are listed above. The harness and its numbers are in the AB-01 hand-off.

## Other GDD targets

AB-02 (actives, re-tunes, Companion/chariot/archer rows) and AB-03 (standard, bearer-killed, RG/Sacred Band) own them. They're **NOT ASSESSED** here, because the effects don't exist yet.

## Decisions (for DECISIONS.md)

1. The new B ids ship as inert data. `autoDevelop` takes them only once they're live (they grant an ability or carry the standard), so AB-01 changes no bot army or ladder floor.
2. Classless (legacy) heroes keep the linear legacy trees.
3. Perk range needs a missile weapon (stats.ts).
4. The substitute B picks are in the table above.
5. The A-vs-B ≤ 6 target is missed in a same-class mirror. It's accepted and re-checked after AB-02 and EC-04.
6. `src/i18n/data.ru.ts` got the 8 new perk names (outside the owned files). It was needed to keep the all-perks translation test green.
