# FO-01 balance: face and focus

`npm run balance` (200 seeds), run on `v5` before the change (b88eadd) and on `fo-01` after it.

## What changed in the sim

- `face` turns a group and keeps its order. A holding group turns at once. A moving group wheels on its centre at 90°/s (foot) or 60°/s (any rider in the group), and it doesn't turn while in melee.
- A `form` whose centre moves less than `formResetDist` (2 paces) keeps the order. A bigger move sets `hold` and ends a fallback.
- `focus`: missile and javelin men shoot the closest man of the focused enemy group who is in range, and otherwise the nearest enemy. Focus clears when the focused group has no man standing.
- The sim now exposes `focusOutOfRange` and `noTargetS` on `SimGroup`.

## New rows (src/dev/ordersBalance.ts)

| Row | Measured | Target | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 hoplites + 2 Companions flanking vs equal-cost hoplites; defender reserve answers with `face` | flanker wins **36%** | flanker ≥ 52% | **Miss, inherited.** The same battle without `face` gives the flanker 30% (42% in classBalance's all-hoplite version), so bot cavalry was already below 52% before FO-01. `face` doesn't erase the flank; here the flanker actually does better (+6 pts) because the reserve that turns to the riders stops supporting the line. The fix belongs to bot cavalry tuning (EC-04, AI stories), not here. |
| Focus on slingers: 6 hoplites + 6 archers (human, holding) vs 6 hoplites + 6 slingers in reserve (bot); time to silence half the slingers | nearest fire 289 s mean (silenced in 6% of battles); focus fire 196 s mean (46%) | ≥ 30% sooner | **Met: 32% sooner.** |

Notes on the focus row. Most nearest-fire battles never silence the slingers, so the medians sit on the 300 s time limit. The row therefore compares mean times censored at the limit, and prints the share of battles that silence them. Silenced means 3 of 6 slingers down or running. With the slingers in the bot's skirmish group (out in front), focus and nearest fire hit the same men and there is no gain, so the row puts them in the reserve, behind the hoplites, which is the case focus is for.
