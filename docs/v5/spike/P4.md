# P4 spike: Phaser 3.90 → Phaser 4.2.1 (S2-P4-SPIKE, decision V5-005)

**Date:** 2026-10-10.
**Branch:** `worktree-agent-a87171f35a803abef`, from `v5` at `b829290`.
**Effort:** about 1.5 h of the 2 h budget.
**ADR:** [`design/adr/ADR-0003-engine-version.md`](../../../design/adr/ADR-0003-engine-version.md), status Proposed.

## Recommendation: UPGRADE, behind a short parity gate

Move v5 to `phaser@^4.2.1` now, before v5 UI and gameplay stories branch.

The migration is much smaller than the audit's estimate of 1–2 weeks:

- 5 commits, about 25 changed lines in 5 source files.
- 0 type errors after the fixes.
- 525/525 unit tests pass.
- The touch smoke test passes 43/43.
- No runtime errors.
- The hub, battle, Kit, world, camp and scrolled-army screens look the same as on 3.90.

Two of the audit's five risk seams really broke, and both are fixed: the hand-built bitmap font and the geometry masks. The other three needed no change: the ScaleManager patch, the canvas texture atlases and the multi-camera `ignore` setup.

Upgrading now is cheaper than later. Every UI line v5 writes on 3.90 would have to be migrated and verified again later. 3.90 is the final Phaser 3 release.

The one thing still open is the cost of the Phaser 4 Mask filter that replaces the geometry masks. In software GL, sheets with a scroll list take 2–3 times longer to open (see "What remains", item 1). With the Metal GPU, the difference was within noise.

**Merge gate:**

1. Make the scroll-area mask cheaper: a low `scaleFactor`, an internal mask, or a camera clip.
2. Clear the layout-check delta in "Measurements".
3. Measure FPS and sheet-open time on a phone.

If the gate is not cleared within 2 days, STAY on 3.90 for v5. ADR-0003 Alternative 1 covers this case.

### What did not break (the audit's seams 1, 4 and 5)

- **ScaleManager `getParentBounds` patch** (`src/platform/renderScale.ts`): works unchanged. The canvas is 780x1688 at DPR 2 on both versions.
- **Canvas textures used as live atlases** (`addCanvas`, `tex.add`, `refresh`): no change needed. All procedural art renders identically.
- **Multi-camera with `cam.ignore`** (battle and world): no change. The world map is pixel-identical apart from water animation.
- **Graphics, tint, tweens and input**: parity. Taps, drags, pinch, the facing knob and ability buttons all pass the smoke test. The code does not use `setTintFill`, which is a no-op in v4.

## What remains

1. **The Mask filter makes sheets slower to open in software GL.** Opening Settings, a docked sheet with a ScrollArea, takes this long until the sheet settles:

   | Build | Software GL (SwiftShader) | Metal GPU |
   |---|---|---|
   | 3.90 | 239–362 ms (3 runs) | 177 ms |
   | 4.2.1 | 586–888 ms (3 runs) | 147–479 ms, noisy, 4 runs |

   The machine was busy with a parallel layout check during these runs, so the absolute numbers are inflated. The gap between versions is not.

   I tried pre-warming one Mask filter in `BootScene`. It did not help: 754–993 ms, so I reverted it. The cost is per Mask instance, not a one-time shader compile. With `filters.external`, each instance allocates and redraws a camera-sized DynamicTexture, here 780x1688 device px.

   The same delay is why the layout check flags `menu-settings`: its 400 ms wait catches the sheet still sliding in.

   Candidate fixes, about 1 d to try and measure:
   - a low `scaleFactor` on `addMask`, since the mask is a plain rectangle;
   - an internal, object-sized mask;
   - or clipping each scroll area with a camera viewport, with no filter at all.

   This is the main unknown for low-end phones.
2. **Layout-check delta**: see the table below. Re-run it after item 1.
3. **Per-frame cost of the Mask filter.** Every scroll area now renders through an extra framebuffer pass. Frame rate with Settings open was not worse on SwiftShader (11.9 fps on 3.90, 14.8 on 4.2.1), but no phone measurement exists yet. Estimated 0.5 d on a mid-range Android device in Telegram.
4. **Text placement**: glyphs land about 1 device px lower and slightly softer: 0.9% of the Kit screen's pixels differ by more than 24/255. See `p4/kit-text-crop-v3-left-v4-right.png`. Cosmetic; it can be accepted or fixed with a half-texel tweak. Estimated 0.25 d.
5. **Screens checked only through the layout check, not by eye**: Duel, Online, Clan, Merchant, BeastTrial, Results and FirstRun. Also the remaining smoke scripts (`duel-smoke`, `online-smoke`, `tutorial-smoke`, `audio-smoke`, `fullscreen-smoke`). Estimated 0.5–1 d.
6. **Canvas renderer fallback** (`Phaser.AUTO` without WebGL): the geometry-mask branch of `screenMask` was not exercised.

**Estimate to finish: 2–3 days**, most of it phone verification and the visual pass. That is well below the audit's 1–2 weeks.

## Measurements (3.90 vs 4.2.1)

All runs were on the same machine. Builds were served by `vite preview`. 3.90 ran on port 5193 and 4.2.1 on port 5194. Port 5183 was already taken by another worktree's dev server, which I left alone.

| `npx vitest run` | 49 files, 525 passed | 49 files, **525 passed** | The sim and server don't import Phaser |
| `npx vite build`, main chunk | 3,102,072 B (gzip 928,210 B) | 3,278,656 B (gzip 967,064 B) | +176.6 kB (+5.7%) minified, +38.9 kB (+4.2%) gzip. Other chunks unchanged. |
