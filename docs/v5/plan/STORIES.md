## Conventions

- **IDs**: `F` foundation kit, `IN` input, `LF` live fix, `ST` sim train / save bump, `AI` doctrines, `FO` formations and orders, `AB` abilities, `AR` campaign arc, `EV` events, `FW` field works, `CT` controls, `RD` readability, `HUB` `TUT` `ARM` `HERO` `BTL` `RES` `WLD` `CMP` `SET` `DUE` `WAR` `SHP` `MKT` `CDX` `DLG` screens, `AS` assets, `FL` feel and audio, `EC` economy, `QA` final QA.
- **Files owned**: the story may edit only these (plus its own new test files and the shared files below). `(new)` means the story creates it. Wildcards own the whole folder for that wave.
- **Shared, append-only files** (no owner; the integrator resolves trivial conflicts): `src/i18n/index.ts` (one import line per new namespace; every story puts its strings in its **own new** `src/i18n/<ns>.en.ts` / `.ru.ts` pair); fixture entries in `scripts/layout-check.mjs` (structure owned by LF-03, then IN-02); a story's own section of `docs/GAMEPLAY.md`, `DESIGN_V2.md`, `DUELS.md`, `ITEMS.md`; `docs/v5/*` state files are the Director's.
- **Not allowed without the Director**: new npm dependencies, `package.json` scripts, `.github/workflows/*` (never `deploy.yml`), anything on `main`.
- **Parallel-safe with**: the stories in the same wave; their file sets are disjoint (checked by script).
- **Estimates**: agent-days, optimistic / **expected** / pessimistic, half-day steps. Not calibrated: no sprint history (estimate skill).
- **Skills**: names resolve to `$F/.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` with `F=/Users/v.gladis/Workspace/Personal/pixelarrow/.claude/worktrees/sd-69ad2c`, except `tdd` → `~/.claude/skills/tdd/SKILL.md`, `design-ship` → `~/.claude/skills/design-ship/SKILL.md`, `frontend-design` → `~/.claude/plugins/marketplaces/anthropic-agent-skills/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md`, `simplify` → the built-in `/simplify`. **Agents**: `$F/.claude/agents/<name>.md`. Full paths per story are in the SPRINTS.md dispatch cards.
- Every story starts with `/story-readiness` against its row and ends with `/story-done` (where listed) and a report ending in **Skills applied**.
