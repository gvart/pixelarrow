This notice explains what Pixelarrow collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how to delete it. It covers the game at play.pixelarrow.app and the website pixelarrow.app.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

The controller of your data is Vladlen Gladis, Germany. For any privacy question or request, write to privacy@pixelarrow.app.

2. What we collect

SourceData
YouYour email address and password (we store only a hash of the password, never the password itself), and what you write to us
Your accountYour display name, the date you joined, and the history of your display names
Your playYour alliance and home region; your men, their gear and their record; your towns, works, goods and coin; your market offers, deals, contracts and gifts; your fights, standings and season scores
TechnicalYour IP address (used only for the moment of the request, for rate limits and the Turnstile bot check; it is not stored with your account), the device and browser family of each signed-in session, and server logs

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, precise location or payment details.

The game keeps you signed in with one cookie, which holds your session. It is needed for the game to work, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Why we use it

  • Running your account and the game: signing you in, saving your progress, running the war, sending the account emails you need, answering your requests. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.
  • Security and abuse prevention: rate limits, bot checks, fair play, stopping banned players from coming back. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a safe, fair game.
  • Server logs: finding and fixing errors. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.

4. What is public

Other players and visitors can see your display name, your standings and season scores, and your alliance. In the game, other players also see your men on the map, the towns and land you hold, and your market offers and contracts.

Some of this also appears in the public war statistics at pixelarrow.app. To be left out of them, write to us.

Display names. Every new account gets a generated name, such as SwiftArcher4821. We never show your email address to other players. Names must follow the game's rules, and a moderator can reset a name that breaks them.

5. Who processes your data

  • Cloudflare, as our processor: hosting the game server and the website, the game's database (Cloudflare D1), sending our emails, and the Turnstile bot check on sign-up, log-in and password reset.

We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: while your account exists. It is deleted when your account is deleted (see section 8).
  • Sessions: up to 30 days, ended after 7 days without use. You can log out sooner.
  • Email links: verification links last 24 hours, password reset links 30 minutes.
  • Database backups: 30 days.
  • Banned players: a one-way hash of the banned email (not the email itself) for 180 days, so the ban can't be avoided with a new account.
  • Your IP address: only for the request itself. Cloudflare's own request logs follow Cloudflare's retention.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • Access your data, or get a copy of it: write to privacy@pixelarrow.app. There is no automatic export yet; we send it by email.
  • Correct it: write to us.
  • Delete it (section 8).
  • Object to a use based on our legitimate interest: write to us.
  • Complain to your data protection authority.

We answer requests within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account

Write to privacy@pixelarrow.app from your account's email address and ask us to delete the account. Deletion can't be undone. We confirm by email when it is done.

Deleted: your email, password hash and display name; your sessions; your men, their gear and record; your goods, coin, towns and holds; your market offers; your alliance membership and season scores. Your email is then free to use for a new account.

Kept, without your name:

  • The war's treasury log and the moderation log, under a pseudonymous number, so the game's economy and moderation can still be checked.
  • Other players' history (deals, contracts, fights), which shows "Deleted player".
  • If you were banned: the email hash described in section 6, for 180 days.

What we can't delete: database backups (gone after 30 days) and Cloudflare's own request logs.

9. Children

You must be at least 16 years old to play Pixelarrow. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Passwords are stored only as a salted, peppered hash (PBKDF2). Links in our emails are single use and expire. Sign-in attempts are rate limited. The session cookie is HttpOnly and Secure, so scripts on the page can't read it and it is only sent over HTTPS.

11. Changes to this notice

We post updates here with a new effective date, and announce important changes in the game or by email. This notice is also available in Russian. If the two versions differ, the English version prevails.
